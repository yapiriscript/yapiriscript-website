Yapiri Kokborok Corpus

Your translation helps preserve Kokborok

Every sentence you translate becomes part of the Yapiri Kokborok Corpus — a living language archive being built to preserve Kokborok for future generations. Your contributions power the dictionary, translator, and eventually Yapiri AI. This dataset is owned by and held in trust for the Tiprasa people.

What is collected: your Yapiri translation  ·  auto-generated Roman Kokborok  ·  domain & difficulty tag  ·  anonymous session ID
What is never collected: your name  ·  email  ·  IP address  ·  any personal data
