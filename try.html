How to Use

01
Schwa vs. Consonant W

W (󱲻) appears word-initially only. Schwa ə (󱲥) appears mid-word only.

󱲻󱲠󱲩 wak — pig
󱲩󱲥󱲨󱲥󱲢 kwtwi — sweet

02
High Tone Mark

Type the vowel first, then tap the high tone key. The mark attaches above the vowel automatically.

󱲪󱲤󱳑󱲷󱲤󱲩 bohrok — they · b·ó·r·o·k

03
Secondary Keys

v, z — loanword characters for borrowed vocabulary and scientific names. v cannot be rendered by b, and z is too distinct from j for scientific names like Zea mays.

For everyday Kokborok writing, 21 native consonants + 6 vowels cover virtually everything.
