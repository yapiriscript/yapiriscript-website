Before you contribute

When you click Contribute, only the Kokborok text you have typed is saved to the Yapiri corpus — an open archive for language research and technology development. We also record your device type and browser language to help us understand how the corpus is being built. We do not collect your name, email, account information, or any data that could identify you personally. Your contribution is entirely anonymous.

How to Use

01
Just Type

Click the output box and type on your keyboard. Each key produces its Yapiri letter — k → 󱲩, a → 󱲠. Backspace, arrows and click-to-position all work normally.

02
Aspirates & High Tone

Hold Shift for aspirated stops: Shift+P → ph, Shift+T → th, Shift+K → kh.

For high tone, type the vowel then press ' (apostrophe).
󱲪󱲤󱳑󱲷󱲤󱲩 bórok — they

03
Key Map Notes

q → schwa (󱲥) · f → ng (󱲵) · c → ch (󱲰) · Shift+N → n′.

This is the same layout as the installable Keyman keyboard — learn it once, use it everywhere.
