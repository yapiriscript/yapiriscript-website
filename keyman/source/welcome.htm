Combining Marks

Diacritics

Diacritics are typed after the base character they modify. Press backspace once to remove the diacritic; press again to remove the base character.

́
High Tone Mark KEY: ' (apostrophe) · U+0301

Marks a syllable as high-pitched. Type the base character first, then press '.

̋
Reduplication Mark KEY: ` (backtick) · U+030B

Marks syllable-level reduplication within a word. For word-level reduplication, follow with the hyphen key.
