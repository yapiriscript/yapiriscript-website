Who Can Join

Everyone is Welcome

Yapiri was built by the community and for the community. Whether you speak Kokborok fluently or are just learning, whether you are a linguist or a schoolchild — there is a place for you here.

"A language without its own script is like a river without a name — it flows, but the map does not know it."
🗣️
Kokborok Speakers

Native speakers are the heart of this project. Your language, your script. Try it, share it, and help us make it better.

📚
Teachers & Educators

Help bring Yapiri into classrooms. We welcome collaboration on teaching materials, worksheets, and learning resources.

✍️
Writers & Poets

Write in Yapiri. Songs, stories, poems — every piece of text written in this script helps establish it as a living writing system.

💻
Developers

Help build keyboard tools, font improvements, mobile apps, or Unicode submission materials. Technical contributions are vital.

🔬
Linguists

Support the Unicode submission process. Help document phonological scope, review the specification, or connect us with institutions.

❤️
Cultural Advocates

Share Yapiri with your network. Every share, every mention, every person who learns this script is a step toward recognition.

The Bigger Goal

Unicode Submission

The long-term mission is to get Yapiri officially encoded in the Unicode Standard — giving Kokborok a permanent place in the global digital text infrastructure, alongside every other major writing system in the world.

This requires demonstrated community use, institutional support, and a formal proposal to the Unicode Technical Committee via the Script Encoding Initiative at UC Berkeley. Community growth is the single most important factor right now.
